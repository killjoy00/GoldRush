Gold Rush Prospecting arrives on Android with the complete current game: Dealt or Drafted scoring cards, Together or Take Turns formats, all 48 scoring cards, Prospector AI with three difficulty levels, Claim Journal, My Claim, career stats, and active-game recovery.

The Android launch also includes adaptive and themed icons, saved setup preferences, leave-game protection, privacy controls, banner ads outside active gameplay, and a one-time Remove Ads purchase.
