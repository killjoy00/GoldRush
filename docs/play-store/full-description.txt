Gold Rush Prospecting is a two-player strategy game built on one simple rule: if you split the claim, your opponent chooses first.

Each round, privately draw mining cards and divide them into two piles. Turn a card face down, then offer the choice. Your opponent takes whichever pile they want. You keep the other.

SPLIT CAREFULLY
Make one pile too good and your opponent will take it. Make the choice close and every face-down card, scoring clue, and bluff starts to matter.

BUILD YOUR CLAIM
Collect Gold Nuggets, Quartz, Ore and Shovels, Gravel and Pans, plus Pack Mules that can fill missing tools. Your six scoring cards decide what your claim is actually worth.

KEEP THEM GUESSING
Some scoring cards are public and some stay secret. Face-down cards in a pile your opponent rejects can stay unknown for the rest of the game. You are never working with perfect information.

DEAL OR DRAFT
Start with six random scoring cards, or draft them from seven-card packs. Drafting gives you more control, but it also tells your opponent more about what you may be chasing.

TWO FORMATS
• Together: both players split, then both choose across four rounds.
• Take Turns: one player splits while the other chooses, then the roles swap across eight rounds.

TWO WAYS TO PLAY
• Pass and play on one Android device.
• Solo against the Prospector, with Steady, Cunning, and Ruthless difficulty levels.

MORE THAN THE FINAL SCORE
Review each round in the Claim Journal, inspect your current tableau in My Claim, track career results by format and scoring family, and browse all 48 scoring cards from the menu.

NO GRIND
No account. No sign-up. No energy timers or virtual currency. Your games, settings, and career stats stay on your device.

A single banner ad appears on the menu. A one-time purchase removes it.

Learn the split in minutes. Spend considerably longer deciding what your opponent thinks you are hiding.
