Privacy Policy

Gold Rush Prospecting · last updated 21 August 2026

The short version. The game itself collects nothing about you and has no accounts, no analytics and no servers. It shows banner ads, and the advertising service that supplies them collects some information. That is the whole story; the rest of this page is the detail.

What the game collects

Nothing. Gold Rush Prospecting has no account system, no login, no analytics, and no server of its own. Your games, settings and results are stored only on your device. We cannot see them, and nothing is transmitted to us.

Advertising

The app displays banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure those ads, Google's advertising software collects information from your device, including:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • device identifiers, such as an advertising identifier
  • advertising data, such as which adverts have been shown
  • app interaction data, such as taps and views
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data

This information is collected by Google, not by us. We never receive it and have no access to it. We see only anonymous, aggregated totals — roughly, how many adverts were shown.

We have configured the app to request non-personalised advertising. The app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and does not do so.

Google's own policy explains what they collect and why: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Game Center

If you choose to play a game online against a friend, the match is carried by Apple Game Center. Apple handles your Game Center identity and the delivery of moves between devices. We do not operate a server, and we never see your Apple ID. Game Center is entirely optional — every other mode works without it.

Apple's handling of that data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can limit ad tracking system-wide in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your device.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored locally.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
