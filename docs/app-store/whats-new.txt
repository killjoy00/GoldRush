The draft is shorter and the decisions are bigger. Packs now open at seven:
take one and pass, then two, then two, and keep one of the last pair while the
other burns face up. Four decisions instead of six, and your own pack comes
back to you — so you find out exactly which two your opponent took from it.

Also new:
• Rematch after an online game. Same opponent, straight into the next one,
  without going back to the menu to re-invite anyone.
• Pick your prospector. Steady plays it straight, Cunning hides the card that
  costs you most to guess wrong, and Ruthless works out what you will do with
  a split before offering it. All three were always in there; now you choose.

Games already in progress finish under the rules they started with.
