Gold Rush Prospecting is now an iPad game as well as an iPhone one — and not
just a stretched phone screen. It rotates, and the layout uses the room.

The two piles now sit side by side, which is how the game actually works: you
are comparing them, not scrolling past one to reach the other. The draft shows
your whole pack of eight at once instead of half of it. Cards are drawn at a
size a thirteen-inch screen deserves.

Also new:
• Remove Ads — a one-time purchase that takes the banner off the menu for
  good. It stays with your Apple Account, so it follows you to a new device.

Everything else is where you left it. Your Claim Journal, career stats and any
game in progress carry across untouched.
