The scoring-card draft is shorter and the decisions in it are bigger.

Packs now open at seven. Take one card as your secret opener and pass the rest,
then two, then two, then keep one and burn the last. Four decisions instead of
six — and the middle of the draft is no longer four near-identical screens.

Your own pack comes back to you with exactly two cards gone, so you learn what
your opponent took from it. They learn the same about you. Only the very first
card either of you takes stays secret, as before.

Dealt games are unchanged, and a game already in progress finishes under the
rules it started with.
